- 自动评估结果含 `score`、`correction`、`reasoning`，关联 evaluator、record、task、experiment（[idl/thrift/coze/loop/observability/domain/annotation.thrift L26-L40](https://github.com/wzqwtt/coze-loop/blob/3a6a2bf07b057fec0c702e514e8345fb5684e83a/idl/thrift/coze/loop/observability/domain/annotation.thrift#L26-L40)）。

- 应用层用 tag 服务校验这条手工标注（[backend/modules/observability/application/trace.go L867-L871](https://github.com/wzqwtt/coze-loop/blob/3a6a2bf07b057fec0c702e514e8345fb5684e83a/backend/modules/observability/application/trace.go#L867-L871)）。
- 创建标注检查 `createLoopTraceAnnotation`，删除检查 `deleteLoopTraceAnnotation`（[backend/modules/observability/domain/component/rpc/auth.go L15-L16](https://github.com/wzqwtt/coze-loop/blob/3a6a2bf07b057fec0c702e514e8345fb5684e83a/backend/modules/observability/domain/component/rpc/auth.go#L15-L16)）。
